Founder & CEO

Alexis Meredith

Alexis Meredith has spent her entire career in healthcare, working hand in hand with physicians to build and grow businesses that improve patient care — work she genuinely loves. She founded Album Capital to bring that same partnership-oriented, operator's mindset to exceptional healthcare practices.

Over more than 15 years, Alexis has built deep experience across healthcare M&A, commercialization and drug development. She has helped build and scale organizations from early-stage companies through major product launches, public-company milestones and strategic transactions.

At her core, Alexis is a builder — creating teams and processes from scratch, bringing structure and calm to complexity, and turning strategy into execution. She has joined organizations as early as employee 15, helped scale businesses through IPO and acquisition, and led teams of up to 200 people.

Through Album Capital, Alexis brings that experience directly to practice owners — preserving what they've built, strengthening the business around them, and thoughtfully building what comes next.

Senior Advisor

Louis Frisina

Louis Frisina is a healthcare executive, entrepreneur and strategic advisor with extensive experience across medical aesthetics, physician practices and healthcare commercialization.

Over his career, Lou has advised and worked with healthcare businesses on growth strategy, commercialization, business development, financing, M&A and operational improvement, with particularly deep experience in the medical aesthetics industry.

At Album Capital, Lou advises on physician partnerships, practice evaluation, transaction strategy, growth initiatives and post-acquisition integration, bringing decades of industry and transaction experience to Album and its practice partners.
